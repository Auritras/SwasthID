Medication–Food Interaction Flags

SwasthID checks your current medication list against a small set of common food or supplement timing rules. These are informational flags, not a substitute for your prescriber or pharmacist.

Packaged Food Barcode Scanner

Scan a packaged-food barcode to look up product nutrition data. If your browser does not support camera barcode scanning, you can enter the barcode manually.

Food Data Source

Indian foods are searched against a curated IFCT 2017 reference subset first; other foods can fall back to USDA FoodData Central. Packaged-food barcode results use Open Food Facts. Values are reference estimates and can vary by preparation, brand, portion, and product formulation.
